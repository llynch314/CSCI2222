Quick and Easy Honey Garlic Chicken

Honey Garlic Chicken Recipe  

Ingredients

Main Ingredients

  • 2 pounts chicken breasts or thighs, cut into cubes
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • black pepper and chile flakes
  • 1/4 cup flour or gluten-free flour
  • 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 cups broccoli florets or chopped asparagus
  • sesame seeds, for serving

Sauce ingredients

  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 6 cloves garlic, grated
  • 3-4 tablespoons chili paste / gochujang
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onion
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil

Directions

  1. Start a pot of rice using a pot or rice cooker
  2. Preheat oven to 450 degrees. Prepare a baking sheet with either parchment paper or oil.
  3. On the sheet pan, mix the chicken, garlic powder, pepper, and chile flakes. Add the flour and toss again. Add 2 tablespoons olive oil, tossing to coat. Arrange the chicken to one side of the baking sheet. Add the broccoli/asparagus to the other side. Toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Bake for 10-15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Mix the sauce in a separate bowl, combining all the sauce ingredients
  5. Remove the vegetables from the sheet pan. Then pour the honey garlic sauce over the chicken and toss the chicken up in the sauce. Bake for another 5 minutes, until the sauce is baked onto the chicken.
  6. Serve the chicken and vegetables ontop of the rice. Top the food with green onions and sesame seeds
